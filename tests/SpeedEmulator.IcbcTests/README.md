# ICBC natural generation and automatic opening balance

Run `dotnet run --project tests/SpeedEmulator.IcbcTests -c Release`.

Automatic opening funding applies to all banks. The amount/date/count
generation algorithm changes only for personal ICBC total-income mode.
Corporate ICBC, other banks and monthly allocation modes keep their generation
algorithms. Negative-balance and explicit opening corrections across all banks
preserve ordinary amounts, dates, IDs, imported rows and transaction numbers.
Generated interest is recalculated after funding; a one-yuan reserve covers the
minimum cumulative balance. A sufficient opening is retained. The same policy
is applied to appended statements before saving.

For personal ICBC references with a positive monthly count, that count means
random attempts in the complete calendar month. Excluded weekends/holidays and
out-of-range draws are discarded without replacement, including partial months.
ICBC checkboxes permit those dates when checked; ordinary weekdays remain valid.
Bundled rest periods cover 2024–2026; other years retain weekday filtering until
their calendar is supplied. Fixed schedules keep their existing date semantics.

Amounts are sampled uniformly over source-valid values at the configured
precision. Income uses the configured total as an optional-income ceiling and
can approach it without inventing illegal denominations. Closing variation is
bounded by source precision rather than a percentage of a large income budget.
Optional expense tail capacity is absorbed by existing optional rows before
resorting to small-only selections. At most one initial optional income transfer
is advanced; other income keeps its sampled dates across the full timeline.
Optional expenses only
fill an unmet spending target. Already selected monthly/fixed expenses are never
compressed to `AllInMoney - LastMoney`. Funding the opening absorbs the shortfall;
ICBC's final balance follows actual cash flows instead of another amount-closing
pass. Ordinary `.01`–`.09` tails remain forbidden, source precision stays valid,
and least-used legal cent suffixes are spread without changing income direction.

The UI skips the legacy final-balance closing rule only in this ICBC mode. It
synchronizes and persists the funded opening for all banks, reports the change,
and uses the combined timeline when appending. Existing saved customer data is
not migrated or regenerated by this code change.

Coverage includes the reported configuration across 30 seeds; uniform full-range
sampling with 2,400 scheduled expenses despite a small budget; dynamic counts;
partial months and date permissions; decimal income/expense directions; interest
and all correction exits; fixed and imported rows; denomination constraints;
zero optional expense budgets; and real generation plus correction across all
34 personal/corporate bank categories. ICBC diagnostic logs omit personal names,
accounts and raw transaction text and record seeds, totals and funded opening.
